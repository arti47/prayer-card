Take the tour

A brief 5-screen intro to what this app is and how the three rhythms — Story Cards, Daily Prayer, Weekly Deep Time — fit together.

Prayer Story Cards

From Paul Miller's A Praying Life — track prayers as ongoing stories. Each card has a Scripture anchor, a two-level ask, and a Father's Perspective line. Pray one card a day, add dated story notes weekly.

Daily Prayer

A daily rhythm — meeting God in His Word and bringing your heart honestly to Him. Three plan options — Morning (25 min), Evening (15 min), Starter (15 min) — each following the same shape: Approach → Bible meditation → Prayer → Contemplation.

Why three plans? Daily prayer can take different shapes depending on the day. Morning is fuller and Bible-led (Approach → Bible reading & meditation → Prayer → Contemplation). Evening is shorter and Psalm-led (Approach → Read a Psalm → Pray through the day). The Starter Plan is a gentler entry point if daily prayer is new (Approach → Meditation → Word Prayer → Free Prayer → Contemplation).

For the deeper, once-a-week practice (Sundays recommended), use Weekly Deep instead — that’s where you map your heart and walk the Lord’s Prayer slowly.

On Approach, name what you’re feeling and Scripture chosen for that feeling appears beneath it — read it, add it to your prayer, or take it as the day’s meditation passage. The same verses are in the Bible reader under Scripture by feeling.

Weekly Deep Time

A once-a-week deeper practice — Sunday recommended. Map your heart, then walk slowly through the Lord’s Prayer with space to listen. Begins with a Preparing to Pray reflection and walks through the Lord's Prayer in 6 steps — each with a Pray prompt and a Listen prompt.

  • ✦ Prepare — Circumstances, feelings (name one, and Scripture for it appears), mapping your heart
  • 1. Come — Worship, draw near
  • 2. Humble — Trust & submission
  • 3. Ask — Bring your needs
  • 4. Repent — Confess & receive forgiveness
  • 5. Forgive — Release others
  • 6. Follow — Dependent obedience

Credits & Source Material

This app is a personal tool. None of the spiritual content is original — it's drawn directly from a study guide and the books behind it.

  • Redemption Hill Church — Practicing The Way: Prayer study guide. The Lord's Prayer flow (Come · Humble · Ask · Repent · Forgive · Follow), the Preparing-to-Pray reflection, the Needs of the Heart chart, and most prompts come directly from this guide.
  • Paul Miller — A Praying Life. The Prayer Story Cards practice — Scripture anchor, two-level ask, Father's Perspective — is built on his work.
  • Tim Keller — Prayer: Experiencing Awe and Intimacy with God. The Daily Prayer practice (the three plan options, with the Approach → Meditation → Prayer → Contemplation shape) is drawn from Ch. 15. Many of the meditation and praise-deepening features across the app — the cry-out affordance, soliloquy mode, Christ-anchor close on Done screens, alternate meditation techniques, and the praise / thanksgiving starter chips — draw from Chapters 10–12.
  • Matthew Henry — A Way to Pray: A Biblical Method for Enriching Your Prayer Life. The  Prayer Library — roughly forty Scripture-saturated written prayers across Praise, Confession, Petition, Thanksgiving, and Concluding — is faithfully drawn from his method.
  • Brené Brown — The Gifts of Imperfection. Two evening prompts take their shape from her daily practice: giving thanks for ordinary, particular moments rather than blessings in general, and naming what we reach for to take the edge off a hard day.
  • English Standard Version (ESV) — Scripture quotations, the  Bible browser, and the Scripture-lookup text are from The Holy Bible, English Standard Version® (ESV®), copyright © 2001 by Crossway, a publishing ministry of Good News Publishers. Used by permission. All rights reserved.

Privacy

Everything you write lives on this device, in this browser. That's it.

  • No accounts. No sign-up, no login, no email.
  • No servers. Your prayers, story cards, and reflections never leave your device.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies for tracking.
  • No third parties. No fonts loaded from CDNs, no scripts from elsewhere — the whole app is in one file.

Because your data is local-only:

  • Clearing your browser cache or site data will erase everything — use  Backup & Restore in the Reflect tab regularly.
  • Moving to a new device means exporting a backup, then importing it on the other end.
  • When this app is deployed to a real URL, its service worker caches only the app files (HTML, icon, manifest). It does not collect or transmit anything you write.

Settings

Appearance

Choose a theme. Auto follows your system setting — useful if you pray morning & evening.

Text size

Top-right of every screen, for quick verse lookup and Scripture by feeling. The inline “Insert from Bible” buttons on verse fields stay either way.

Markdown Export

Export your prayer cards and sessions as Markdown — drop the files into Obsidian, Logseq, Bear, or any markdown editor. Each export uses YAML frontmatter, structured headings, and [[wikilinks]] so notes connect properly in Obsidian's graph view.

Bulk export

Each download is a single combined .md file with --- dividers between entries. Use Obsidian’s “Note Composer” plugin (or split manually) to break it into one note per item.

Obsidian vault (optional)

If you set your vault name here, future per-item Obsidian export buttons would route into your vault directly. Currently used only for the filename hint in exports.

Backup & Restore

Your data lives in this browser only. Back it up regularly so you can restore on another device or after clearing cache. Backups include all prayer cards, Pray & Listen sessions, story notes, heart-need selections, linked cards, and streak history.
